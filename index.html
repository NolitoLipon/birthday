Happy, happy birthday, Mommy Jen! 🥳🎂❤️

First of all, I just want to take this opportunity to express how thankful I am that I got the chance to meet you and eventually call you my Mommy. Who would've thought that meeting someone on TikTok would turn into something this meaningful? What started as a simple interaction eventually became a bond that I genuinely treasure. We may not be related by blood, but over time, you became someone I could call family, and I'm really grateful that life gave me the chance to know you. Out of all the people I could've met on that app, I'm so glad that one of them was you. 🥹❤️

It's also crazy to think that we've already spent a year together. During that time, I've had the chance to see even just a little piece of the kind and beautiful soul that you have. I've seen how caring you are, how much you value the people around you, and how you always find ways to make people feel loved and appreciated. A year may not seem like a very long time, but it was enough for me to realize how lucky I am to have met someone like you. We've already shared so many memories, laughter, random moments, and conversations, and I hope this is only the beginning of many more years together. ❤️

One of the things I admire most about you is your ability to still think about the people you love even when you're going through something yourself. I know you've been through things that weren't easy, and you also have your own battles that you may not always talk about. Yet somehow, you still manage to think about us, check on us, support us, and make sure we're okay. 🥹 Sometimes, I honestly wonder how you still have enough love left to give when you're carrying things of your own. I hope you know that you don't always have to be strong for everyone. You deserve to have people who will take care of you, listen to you, and stay beside you during the times when you're the one who needs support. ❤️

I'm also really grateful to be part of the JENatics Family. Thank you for creating a space where people who started as strangers could somehow become a family. We've shared laughter, jokes, random conversations, unforgettable moments, and even difficult times together. We may be a little crazy and funny sometimes 😭😂, but that's also what makes our family special. No matter what, I'm really thankful for the bond we have and all the memories we've made together. It's amazing how people from different places and different walks of life found each other because of you. I hope you know that you've created something more meaningful than you probably realize. 🥹❤️

On your birthday, I pray that God continues to bless you with good health, peace of mind, genuine happiness, and many more years to celebrate with the people who love you. I hope He gives you strength whenever life becomes difficult, guides you through every decision you make, and helps you heal from the things you don't always talk about. I hope this new chapter of your life brings you answered prayers, new opportunities, beautiful memories, and more reasons to wake up every day feeling grateful and excited for what's ahead. Most importantly, I hope you receive the same love, kindness, and care that you've given so freely to other people.

And, of course, I have one more very important birthday wish for you...

Mommy, I sincerely pray that God finally sends you a boyfriend. 😭🙏🏻😂 HAHAHAHA! But wait... is it Kuya Isaac already? 👀 Or Daddychan Pogi? YIEEEEE! 😭😂 HAHAHAHA! Mommy, maybe we're out here waiting for someone when the plot twist has been right in front of us this whole time. 👀😂 Just kidding! HAHAHAHA!

But kidding aside, Mommy, whoever you choose to be with someday, please know that the triplets will always be here to support you. ❤️ What matters most to us is seeing you genuinely happy and being with someone who treats you with the love, respect, and care you deserve. We just want someone who will bring you peace, make you smile, stay by your side through the good and difficult days, and love you for who you truly are.

So whoever that person may be, as long as you're happy and you're being treated right, your triplets will always be here cheering you on and supporting you. 🥹❤️ Of course, we'll still need to do a little background check first... you know, just for quality assurance purposes. 😭✋🏻😂 HAHAHAHA!

And with that, I just want to say once again how thankful I am for you, Mommy. 🥹❤️ In just one year, you've become such a special part of my life, and I'm grateful for every memory, laugh, and moment we've shared together. Thank you for being my Mommy, for bringing our JENatics Family together, and for still caring about us even when you're fighting battles of your own. I hope this new year of your life brings you the happiness, peace, good health, love, and blessings that you truly deserve. Never forget that you don't have to face everything alone because you have people who genuinely love and care about you, including your triplets, who will always be here for you.

I love you so much, Mommy Jen. 🥹❤️ God bless you always, and once again, happy, happy birthday! 🥳🎂❤️
